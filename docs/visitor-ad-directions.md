# Visitor sign-in: five graphic directions

The look comes from the moodboard. Every piece is a 4:5 or 9:16 feed ad on one flat, loud colour (cobalt blue, orange, egg-yolk yellow, acid lime or bright sky). The people are shot like a fashion campaign, with hard sun or flash, crisp shadows, rich skin, close or low angles and a wide lens, and they push the thing we're selling (the tablet, the phone, the gate book) out towards the viewer. Expressions are big. Clothes are picked to clash with or echo the background. Type is fat grotesque or condensed poster type set huge, often behind or cropped by the person, with tiny supporting copy and the odd serif or script word for contrast. Real-looking UI pieces (notification cards, toggles, chips) sit on the photo. The voice is cheeky Nigerian-friendly, talking to you like a friend.

Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5, Flare) does only the people, shot on a plain backdrop in the layout colour so they cut out cleanly. Type, UI and layout get built on top.

## 1. The door

This one is like the T-rex and tiger door pins, but in colour. A white freestanding door sits on flat cobalt blue, and a sharp young visitor in a tangerine suit and sunglasses strides out of it towards the camera from a low angle, with a hard sun shadow behind. A small sign-in tablet is mounted on the door frame. The headline is heavy black type: "Who just walked in?"

**Person:** the visitor mid-stride, low angle, hard light, on cobalt blue.

## 2. Giant word, one person

This is like Sendit, Otega and Roaming. On cobalt blue, "WHO'S IN?" is set in huge condensed white letters cropped off both edges. The receptionist is cut out in front, laughing, and pushes the sign-in tablet right into the lens with a wide lens so it fills the bottom of the frame. She has braids, gold hoops and a yellow blazer. The real kiosk screen goes onto the tablet.

**Person:** the receptionist in a yellow blazer pushing a blank tablet at the lens, on flat blue.

## 3. Your guest is here

This is like Pill4rs, Cowrywise and MRG. On flat orange, the host holds his phone up to the camera with a delighted, open-mouthed reaction, lit by hard flash. The real email card, "Amaka Obi is here to see you", floats next to the phone with a "Visiting you" chip. The headline is "Your guest is here. You already know."

**Person:** the host in a cobalt shirt on orange, phone up to the lens, big reaction.

## 4. Don't buy a new visitor book

This is like the serum ad, but louder. On egg-yolk yellow, a big black serif headline reads "DON'T BUY A NEW VISITOR BOOK." A security guard's hand pushes a battered gate book with a biro on a string straight at the lens, as if he's offering it to you. The small copy says "Unless you enjoy reading 'A. Bakar?' at 9:15", with three tick chips along the bottom.

**Person:** the guard's hand and sleeve holding the book at the lens, hard flash, on yellow.

## 5. Neon carousel

This is like Nova Studio and Social Media Vol 4: five cards in black and acid lime with chunky type, one point per card. Card 1 says "Who's in your building right now?", card 2 shows the gate book with "You can't read this", card 3 shows the tablet tap with "10 seconds to sign in", card 4 shows the email card with "Tunde knows before she sits", and card 5 says "Free for 30 days." The photos are high-contrast black and white cutouts.

**Person:** a black-and-white flash portrait of the visitor, finger reaching towards the lens to tap, on plain grey.
