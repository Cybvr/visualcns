[TITLE]: KNOW WHO'S IN

[ABOUT]: VisualCNS Pass is visitor sign-in for offices. It replaces the paper visitor book at the front desk. Visitors sign in on an iPad at the desk or by scanning a QR code with their phone. They sign any forms the company needs on screen before they walk in. The person they're visiting gets a message straight away. Visitors only get through the doors they're allowed through. In a fire alarm, one tap shows who is still inside. Every visit is saved, so audits take minutes. VisualCNS sends a ready-to-go iPad stand: plug it in, connect to Wi-Fi, done. Free for 30 days, no card needed.

[SCENE SUMMARY]: A 60-second, 9:16 vertical VisualCNS film for Pass, made like an infographic. Frames switch hard between white and black. Each idea gets one big word that fills the frame, then a simple diagram that explains it, with numbered callouts, leader lines, counts and small icons. One thick line runs through the whole film and ties it together.

[SUBJECT & SETTING]:
SUBJECT: Pass itself, shown through simple diagrams and its screens: the paper book, a top-down office floor plan, the iPad sign-in screen, a phone with a QR code, an on-screen form, the host message card, door labels, a who's-inside list, a visit search, and the iPad stand.
SETTING: No people and no rooms. Flat white frames with black type, and flat black frames with white type. VisualCNS blue is the only colour, used for the line and anything Pass does. Red is used once, for the fire alarm.

[VISUAL DEVICE]: THE LINE. One thick line runs through every scene, and a small dot rides along it as the visitor.
- In the paper book it is a ruled line with scrawled names on it. It gets scribbled over and tangled.
- When Pass arrives it snaps straight and turns blue.
- From then on it is the visitor's route through a top-down floor plan: front door, then the iPad, then the forms, then the host, then the doors they're allowed through.
- In the fire alarm it turns red and leads to the exit.
- At the audit it stretches out into a timeline of saved visits.
- At the end it draws an underline under "Know who's in your building."
