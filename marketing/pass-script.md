[TITLE]: KNOW WHO'S IN

[ABOUT]: VisualCNS Pass is visitor management for offices. It replaces the paper visitor book at the front desk. Visitors sign in on an iPad or by scanning a QR code with their phone. They sign NDAs, safety waivers and other forms on screen before they walk in. Hosts are pulled from Okta, Microsoft Azure AD or Google Workspace, and get a message on Slack, Microsoft Teams or SMS when their visitor arrives. Visitors get a temporary RFID badge or mobile pass that only opens the doors they're allowed through, via Brivo, Lenel S2, Genea or Kisi. Every visit is saved, so audits take minutes. VisualCNS sends a ready-to-go iPad stand: plug it in, connect to Wi-Fi, done. Free for 30 days, no card needed.

[SCENE SUMMARY]: A 60-second, 9:16 vertical VisualCNS film for Pass, made like an infographic. Frames switch hard between white and black. Each idea gets one big word that fills the frame, then a simple diagram that explains it, with numbered callouts, leader lines, counters and the logos of the tools it works with. One thick line runs through the whole film and ties it together.

[SUBJECT & SETTING]:
SUBJECT: Pass itself, shown through simple diagrams and its screens: the paper book, a top-down office floor plan, the iPad sign-in screen, a phone with a QR code, an NDA on screen, the host message, a badge and doors, a visit search, and the iPad stand.
SETTING: No people and no rooms. Flat white frames with black type, and flat black frames with white type. VisualCNS blue #0E32FC is the only colour, used for the line and anything Pass does. Red is used only for the risk tags on the paper book.

[VISUAL DEVICE]: THE LINE. One thick line runs through every scene, and a small dot rides along it as the visitor.
- In the paper book it is a ruled line with a name on it. It gets scribbled over and tangled.
- When Pass arrives it snaps straight and turns blue.
- From then on it is the visitor's route through a top-down floor plan: front door, then the iPad, then the forms, then the host, then the doors they're allowed through.
- At the audit it stretches out into a timeline of saved visits, with one tick per visit.
- At the end it draws an underline under "Know who's in your building."
