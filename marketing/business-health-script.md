[TITLE]: BUSINESS HEALTH

[ABOUT]: Business Health is a VisualCNS feature that gives a business a full check-up in about two minutes. It reads the company's record, crawls its website and runs eight public searches: competitors, competitor news, industry trends, events, tenders, grants, partners, and mentions and reviews. It then produces a health score out of 100, the problems that need attention, the best-fit opportunities, the market and competitors, how the business looks online, changes since the last scan, and a prioritised action plan with next steps. Owners can also ask questions about the report and get sourced answers.
